Welcome to Course Organizer!

Course Organizer is a web application designed to help students manage their courses and stay organized throughout the semester.

View your courses, Add new ones and much more!

Course Management

Easily add, edit, and delete courses to keep track of your schedule.

Organized Layout

A clean and intuitive interface that allows you to quickly access your course information.

Contact Support

Have questions or need assistance? Contact our support team for help.

How It Works

Step 1: Add Courses

Use the "Add Course" page to input your course details and save them to your schedule.

Step 2: View Courses

Navigate to the "My courses" page to see a list of all your added courses and their details.

Step 3: Stay Organized

Use the organized layout to quickly access your course information and stay on top of your schedule.
